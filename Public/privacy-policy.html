1. Overview

BullsGo is a social network for investors. We are committed to protecting your personal data and complying with applicable privacy laws worldwide.

This policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over your information. It applies to all users of the BullsGo mobile and web applications.

Data controller: BullsGo (contact details in Section 10).

🗃️

2. Data We Collect

Account data

  • Name, email address, and password (hashed)
  • Profile photo, bio, location (optional)
  • Investment plan and subscription tier

Usage data

  • Posts, comments, likes, and shares you create
  • Portfolio assets you add (stored only on our servers)
  • Screens visited and features used

Technical data

  • IP address, device type, operating system
  • Browser language and region preference
  • Push notification token (if you grant permission)

Payment data

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. BullsGo never stores card numbers or banking details.

🍪

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Essential cookies

Required for authentication, security, and core app functions. These cannot be disabled.

Analytics cookies

Anonymous data used to understand how users interact with the app and identify areas for improvement. We use Supabase analytics — no third-party ad trackers.

Preference cookies

Store your language, region, currency, and theme settings in localStorage so they persist between sessions.

Managing cookies

You can withdraw or change your cookie consent at any time from Settings → Appearance → Cookie Preferences. You can also clear cookies via your browser settings, though this will log you out.

4. EU & UK — GDPR Rights

GDPR UK GDPR
If you are located in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the following rights under GDPR / UK GDPR.

Your rights

  • Right to access — request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your account and data.
  • Right to restriction — limit how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent for non-essential cookies at any time.

Legal bases for processing

  • Contract — providing the BullsGo service you signed up for.
  • Consent — non-essential cookies and marketing communications.
  • Legitimate interests — fraud prevention and service security.

Data retention

Account data is retained until you delete your account. Anonymised analytics data may be retained for up to 24 months.

Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (e.g., ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

5. Brazil — LGPD

LGPD
Se você está no Brasil, a Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD — Lei nº 13.709/2018) garante os seguintes direitos.

Seus direitos (Art. 18 LGPD)

  • Confirmação da existência de tratamento dos seus dados
  • Acesso aos dados que temos sobre você
  • Correção de dados incompletos, inexatos ou desatualizados
  • Anonimização, bloqueio ou eliminação de dados desnecessários
  • Portabilidade dos dados para outro serviço ou produto
  • Eliminação dos dados tratados com seu consentimento
  • Informação sobre com quem compartilhamos seus dados
  • Revogação do consentimento a qualquer momento

Base legal para tratamento

  • Execução de contrato — prestação dos serviços do BullsGo.
  • Consentimento — cookies não essenciais e comunicações de marketing.
  • Legítimo interesse — segurança e prevenção a fraudes.

Encarregado de Dados (DPO)

Para exercer seus direitos ou esclarecer dúvidas, entre em contato pelo e-mail indicado na Seção 10.

6. California — CCPA / CPRA

CCPA
California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Your rights

  • Right to know — what personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell.
  • Right to delete — request deletion of your personal information.
  • Right to opt out — opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. BullsGo does not sell your personal information.
  • Right to correct — correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Categories of information collected

Identifiers, commercial information (subscription tier), internet activity (app usage), and inferences drawn from usage data.

How to submit a request

Email us at the address in Section 10. We will respond within 45 days as required by law.

7. Data Sharing

We only share your data with trusted third parties strictly necessary to operate BullsGo:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and real-time services (EU data centres).
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Vercel — web hosting and edge network (US/EU).
  • Anthropic — AI features (BullsAI chat). Prompts are not used to train models.

We never sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

🔒

8. Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plaintext.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Optional multi-factor authentication (MFA) via TOTP.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced on all database tables.
  • Regular security reviews and dependency audits.
👶

9. Children's Privacy

BullsGo is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account.
